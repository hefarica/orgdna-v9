## What the count means

The original 397-row inventory mixed fields, buttons, links, disclosures and dynamic repeated families. Its 306 MANUAL entries were not 306 independent workflows. The revised inventory preserves every one of the 381 HTML control rows, repairs three dynamic family selectors and adds six previously missing dynamic families. It has 403 rows: 179 with authored assertions and 224 still requiring dedicated verification. All actual browser statuses remain NOT_RUN.

The remaining 224 rows comprise 92 fields/options, 35 disclosures, 3 hidden IDs, 22 links, 64 actions and 8 repeated dynamic families. A form’s name, description and evidence fields can be exercised together in one transaction, but serialization and field-specific validation still need assertions. Selecting a single option does not cover the other branches. Repeated cards can share one handler case with additional identity/selection checks; a repeated Renew button and a Release button are different transitions.

## Distinct scenarios versus shared controls

- Configuration: serialization is shared; source kind, template, provider, orchestration, autonomy and numeric ranges are separate branches. Real credentials/persistence remain separate from sentinel-only memory checks.
- Project/profile/subteam: save/load/copy is one version workflow. Supervisor edits, dirty dismissal, stale changes, tool-policy inheritance and facilitator/member restrictions need their own assertions.
- Governance: deliverable, independent reviewer filtering, correction/history, score, eligibility, proposal and supervisor/Owner decision are distinct transitions. New cases explicitly distinguish manual declarations from authenticated decisions or applied authority.
- Coordination: acquire, renew, release, overlap and writer limits have new cases. Expiry, stale concurrent versions and cross-session behavior remain gaps.
- Council: text fields belong to an entry transaction; changing members/sessions, decision records, participation coverage and closure are separate cases.
- Capabilities: library creation/edit-copy/search belong to storage; all/selected/subteam targets and assign/activate/deactivate/unassign are independent axes. New tests assert selected recipient IDs, overlapping subteam deduplication, changed-target review invalidation, exact plan hash, inactive assignments and preserved immutable originals. Full cross-product and pagination remain pending.
- Skills/feedback/remediation: fields share record saves, but feedback versions, cancellation, skipped transitions, review independence and final closure are separate. New cases cover feedback v1/v2/v3 persistence and early remediation transitions, not the whole review chain.
- Catalog/resources/evaluation: recommendation consent, per-agent mapping, catalog identity/version changes, install-document/manual reports and evaluation command execution/cancel have distinct risks. These need explicit inert adapters or authorized real-integration runs. They remain unverified.
- Exports/references/disclosures: identical evidence-download payloads can share content checks; recipe, definitions, organization, Council/lifecycle and ZIP/Cordis outputs each need format-specific assertions. Every link still needs a usable location. Disclosure mechanics are shared, while reachability under nested panels/tabs is per location.

Presence in this inventory does not mean their behavior is covered. Exact case mappings and unresolved variants remain in the JSON matrix.
